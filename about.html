ABOUT THIS ASSISTANT

What Is This Website?

FuelForm helps active individuals organize their daily nutrition around exercise without overwhelming them with complicated calorie counting or rigid diets.

PRE-WORKOUT

What should I eat before training?

Easily digestible carbohydrates paired with light protein 60–90 minutes before your workout to sustain energy levels.

POST-WORKOUT

What should I eat after training?

High-quality protein (25-40g) combined with carbohydrates within 2 hours to accelerate muscle repair and restore glycogen.

REST DAYS

What can I eat on a rest day?

Balanced protein and nutrient-dense whole foods with moderate carbs to support tissue recovery and active repair.

HIGH PROTEIN

What meals are high in protein?

Lean chicken breast, salmon, eggs, Greek yogurt, quinoa, legumes, and tofu tailored for maximum muscle synthesis.

TIME SAVING

What can I prepare quickly?

5-minute protein smoothies, tuna rice bowls, and 10-minute turkey avocado wraps designed for busy training schedules.

HABIT BUILDING

How can I stay consistent?

By keeping food choices simple, predictable, and aligned with your daily workout routine instead of overthinking.

Simple food decisions → better consistency → better support for training and recovery.

OUR METHODOLOGY

4 Pillars of Athlete Nutrition

Built on evidence-informed sports nutrition principles designed to fit naturally into your busy lifestyle.

⏱

1. Timing Over Extreme Counting

Fueling your body 60-90 minutes before training and within 2 hours after is significantly more influential for muscle retention and energy than meticulous calorie tracking.

🥩

2. High Bioavailability Protein

We prioritize complete amino acid profile foods—like eggs, poultry, wild seafood, and Greek yogurt—to stimulate muscle protein synthesis efficiently.

⚡

3. Carbohydrate Periodization

Scale your carbohydrate intake to match workout intensity. Higher carb intake on heavy session days; moderate carbs on recovery and light training days.

💧

4. Fluid & Electrolyte Discipline

Maintain consistent hydration throughout the day. Dehydration of just 2% body mass reduces strength, power, and cognitive focus during training.
